C1.1 Matcha Latte

Classic matcha 🍵 soft serve with a hint of honey, and macros so good that you can eat the whole pint. One of my favourites so far! This really is the perfect treat. Taste - 4.5/5★

Recipe

Ingredients for a 24-oz pint:

  • Matcha powder (1 ½ tbsp)
  • Milk, 0% fat, ultra-filtered (1 ½ cups)
  • Silken tofu (212.5 g)
  • Salt (⅛ tsp)
  • Honey (1 tbsp)
  • Monkfruit sweetner with allulose (⅓ cup)
  • Xanthan gum (¼ tsp)
  • Vanilla extract, pure (1 tsp)

Instructions:

  1. Blend everything together.
  2. Freeze for 24 hours.
  3. Spin on LITE ICE CREAM (push down and re-spin if needed).

Reflections

Overall Rating: ★★★★★

Wow! This pint was so tasty and the macros are amazing! It tastes just like a matcha latte soft-serve. This one is going on Celine’s Choice.

The Good (what went well):

  • Not too sweet: ⅓ cup sweetner with honey sounds like a lot but it turned out really balanced. The floral honey elevated the earthiness of the matcha.
  • Very mucha matcha: The matcha taste was strong! Matcha lovers can add ½–1 tbsp more for an extra-rich pint.

The Bad (lessons learned):

  • A smidge too beany: The tofu taste was a bit stronger than I envisioned, and it tasted like a matcha latte with soy-milk substitute. Add less tofu for the next one and some more fat.
