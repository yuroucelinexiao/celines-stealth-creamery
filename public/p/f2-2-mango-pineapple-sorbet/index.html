F2.2 Mango Pineapple Sorbet

The perfect summer sorbet! Loaded fresh mangoes 🥭 with some pineapple chunks 🍍 for the ultimate tropical swirl. Taste-5/5★

Recipe

Ingredients for a 24-oz pint:

  • Mango, fresh (3 medium / 384 g)
  • Pineapple, frozen (140 g)
  • Mango juice (177 ml)
  • Honey (½ tbsp)

Instructions:

  1. Blend everything together.
  2. Freeze for 24 hours.
  3. Spin on SORBET (push down and re-spin if needed).
