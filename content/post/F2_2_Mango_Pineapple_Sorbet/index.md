## Recipe

**Ingredients for a 24-oz pint:**
- Mango, fresh (3 medium / 384 g)
- Pineapple, frozen (140 g)
- Mango juice (177 ml)
- Honey (&frac12; tbsp)

**Instructions:**
1. Blend everything together.
2. Freeze for 24 hours.
3. Spin on SORBET (push down and re-spin if needed).
